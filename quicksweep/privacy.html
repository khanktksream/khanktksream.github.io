QuickSweep Launcher Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickSweep Launcher handles information when you use its cleanup, media review, app-management, and device-information features.

1. Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you choose and the permissions available on your device, QuickSweep Launcher may process:

  • file and storage information, such as file type, size, path, and whether an item may be removable;
  • media information and thumbnails needed to show images or videos for your review;
  • installed-app information, such as app name, icon, size, and installation details;
  • device and battery information, such as charge level, capacity, temperature, technology, and voltage; and
  • basic diagnostics needed to keep the app reliable, such as app version, device model, operating-system version, and crash details.

2. How information is used

Information is used to display scan results, help you review storage and media, show device or battery details, carry out actions you select, troubleshoot problems, and improve reliability. QuickSweep Launcher does not delete media, files, or apps unless you initiate and confirm the relevant action through the app or the operating system.

3. Permissions and local processing

The app may request access to media, files, installed-app information, or device details when a feature requires it. Permission prompts are controlled by your operating system, and you may deny or revoke permissions in device settings. Core analysis is designed to occur on the device whenever practical.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be disclosed to infrastructure, diagnostics, or support providers only when needed to operate, secure, or maintain the app. It may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards. Personal information is not sold for monetary payment.

5. Retention

On-device scan results remain under your control and are removed according to device behavior, app settings, or uninstallation. Any limited diagnostic or support information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, security, and dispute resolution.

6. Your choices

You can manage app permissions in Android settings, choose which cleanup or management actions to take, clear app data, or uninstall the app. You may contact us to ask about privacy requests that apply in your region.

7. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children

QuickSweep Launcher is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
