Last updated: November 20, 2026

App: LostFile · Package: com.lostphotovideo.android.app

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LostFile (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use LostFile, package com.lostphotovideo.android.app, and contact our support team. The features available to you may depend on your device and app version. This policy covers file recovery and media-management features, including optional notification-based message recovery where offered.

2. File access and permissions

When you choose a recovery or media-management feature, the app requests the access needed to scan accessible files, display previews and details, save selected recovered items, or delete items you select for cleanup. Depending on Android version and the feature, this may involve storage access, photo/video/audio permissions, a system file picker, or All files access. Granting access does not mean that Android makes every deleted file recoverable. You may deny or revoke access in Android settings; related features may then be unavailable. Review selections before confirming deletion.

3. Photos, videos and other files

Recovery scans may examine file contents and associated details such as file name, path, type, size, timestamps and thumbnails. Under the local-recovery model described by this policy, scanning and recovery take place on your device; photos, videos, audio and other recovered files are not uploaded to our servers as part of recovery. Selected recovered items are saved locally. If you choose to share or export a file to another app or service, that destination handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. This local-recovery statement does not describe the separate technical information processed by advertising or diagnostic services.

4. Optional notification-based message recovery

If you enable message recovery and grant Notification access, the app can process notification content made available by Android from supported messaging apps. This can include the source app, sender name displayed in the notification, message preview and timestamp. A local history may be retained so you can review content after a notification or message is removed. This feature does not bypass end-to-end encryption, read private message databases, or retrieve content that Android did not expose in notifications. It cannot reconstruct messages received before access was enabled. Notification content and recovered notification media are not uploaded to our servers under this model. You can stop future access in Android’s Notification access settings and remove retained local history using available app or system controls.

5. Device, usage and diagnostic information

Technical information may be processed to operate the service, measure feature use, deliver and measure advertising, detect misuse and diagnose failures. Depending on the services active in your version and your choices, this may include device model, Android version, language, time zone, app and SDK versions, advertising or other device identifiers permitted by platform rules, network information, IP address, interaction events and crash logs. Technical logs may describe the app state and time of an error. File-recovery access is separate from these technical-data activities.

6. Information you send to support

When you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, investigate your request and maintain relevant support records. Please avoid sending private files, credentials or notification contents unless they are necessary for your request.

8. Sharing and service providers

We may use service providers to support app operation, diagnostics, advertising and user support. Information is made available for the relevant service purposes and subject to appropriate obligations. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable notice and safeguards. User-initiated sharing is controlled by the destination you select. We do not sell recovered file contents or notification histories.

9. Retention and deletion

Local recovery results, notification history and caches remain on your device until removed by the app’s controls, Android storage controls or other device actions. Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally removes app-private data but may not remove recovered or exported files saved in shared storage; delete those separately with a file manager. Revoking permission stops future access but does not necessarily erase data already saved. Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to address requests and meet applicable obligations. Third-party retention is governed by the relevant provider’s practices and applicable law.

Where the app offers a privacy or consent control, you can use it to update your preferences. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent or request portability. Contact us to exercise applicable rights or ask about information handling. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

This standalone product website does not embed advertising, analytics, tracking scripts or third-party fonts. Following an external link opens a separate service with its own practices.

Third-party services and support infrastructure may process information outside your country, subject to applicable legal requirements and safeguards. Keep your device protected and review the recipients of any files you choose to share.

13. Children’s privacy
